# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the work: bugs, ideas, code and docs.
- Respect that people have different experience and different ways of working.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who do so repeatedly or seriously.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" link on the comment, or open a private report through the repository's Security tab. Reports are handled privately, and reporters will not face retaliation.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project in public.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
